# T-012 — Scrum practices

- **Summary:** borrow more Scrum into the sprint loop — story points, a sprint retro, backlog refinement — where it pays for a solo agent-driven project
- **Category:** feature
- **Importance:** medium
- **Effort:** XL
- **Depends on:** none

## Why

The kanban and sprint skeleton exists, but estimation is a coarse S–L label, nothing feeds lessons
from one sprint into the next, and Draft tickets only get refined when `open-sprint` happens to pull
them. Scrum has proven answers to all three; the open question is which survive the move to a solo
developer working with agents.

## What

Decide per practice — adopt, adapt or reject — and wire the survivors into the workflow skills:

- **Story points** — replace or complement the S–L effort scale; velocity tracking across sprints?
- **Sprint retro** — a step in `close-sprint`; where its outcome lands (memory, `docs/dev.md`, the
  workflow skills themselves).
- **Backlog refinement** — a recurring pass (own skill, or a `shape` mode) that sharpens Draft
  tickets into S–L Backlog tickets.

Split into one ticket per adopted practice before it leaves Draft.
